Bio

Room Full Of Oscillators — a synthesizer project

Long before software instruments and streaming platforms, there were cheap cassette decks, late-night radio broadcasts, and synthesizers humming in dimly lit rooms. We grew up in the Netherlands during the 1980s and 90s, surrounded by the rise of electronic music, home recording culture, and the unmistakable sound of magnetic tape.

Our project continues that lineage through analog synths, tape saturation, drifting textures, and slow-moving compositions that feel both distant and strangely familiar.

Recorded in the Netherlands — home of Philips, the company that introduced the compact cassette tape to the world.
